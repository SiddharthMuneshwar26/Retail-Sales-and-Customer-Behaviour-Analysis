# Retail-Sales-and-Customer-Behaviour-Analysis
Project Description

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to uncover meaningful insights into customer purchasing behavior, product performance, sales trends, pricing impact, and customer retention patterns. The analysis was performed using Python and various data analysis libraries to simulate real-world business intelligence and retail analytics workflows.

The objective of the project is to answer key business questions that can help retail businesses better understand their customers, optimize sales strategies, improve customer retention, and identify revenue-driving opportunities.

The dataset was cleaned, processed, and analyzed using exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation techniques, and churn risk analysis. Multiple visualizations and KPI-based summaries were created to present insights in a clear and business-oriented manner.

Key Business Questions Analyzed
Q1: Who are our most valuable customers?

Identified high-value customers based on total spending, transaction frequency, and purchasing behavior.

Q2: How does customer age and gender influence purchasing behavior?

Analyzed demographic trends to understand how different customer groups contribute to sales and purchasing patterns.

Q3: Which product categories drive the most revenue?

Evaluated product category performance to determine the strongest contributors to total revenue and customer demand.

Q4: Monthly trends in sales and transactions

Explored seasonal and monthly sales trends to identify fluctuations in customer activity and transaction volume over time.

Q5: What are the patterns in purchase quantity per transaction?

Examined customer purchasing volume and basket-size behavior to better understand transaction-level buying patterns.

Q6: How does pricing affect purchasing behavior?

Investigated the relationship between pricing, quantity purchased, and customer spending behavior.

Q7: Customer Retention and Activity Analysis

Developed a churn risk analysis model using customer recency, purchase frequency, transaction volume, and spending trends to identify customers at risk of becoming inactive.
